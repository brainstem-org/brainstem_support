{%- comment -%} The landing-page tour: one short silent clip per key feature, each made of shots. The tour opens on a welcome card (`intro` in the data: what BrainSTEM is) and goes straight into the first feature. A chapter opens on a title card (its number, title and one line on what it shows), held long enough to read (a click skips it); the player then plays the whole chapter through, each later shot opening on a short banner along the bottom of the picture naming what it shows (the clip holds just long enough to read it; a click skips it), holds the last frame for a moment and goes on to the next chapter, so left alone the tour plays once from start to end and waits on the last chapter. Next opens the following chapter at once, Back the previous one, Replay plays this one again; the arrow keys do the same while the tour has focus, and any of them stops the automatic advance for that chapter. The captions are not part of the clips: each chapter's cues (seconds into its clip and a sentence, one per shot) are shown under the video as it reaches them, the caption flashing as it changes, and the progress bar has a segment per shot. The list that follows the include, marked `{: .tour-features }`, becomes the chapter picker (one item per chapter, in order): clicking an item plays its clip, and the playing one's number is filled in. Without such a list, a row of chapter buttons under the video does the same. Chapters come from docs/_data/tour.yml and the clips and posters from docs/assets/images/walkthroughs/tour/, all written by `python scripts/screenshots.py tour`.
