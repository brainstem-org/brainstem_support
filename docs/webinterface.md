# Web interface

The web interface is the primary way to work with BrainSTEM. You enter, browse, filter, export and share your experimental metadata from the browser. Two portals share the same interface: the private portal for your own and shared data, and the public portal for data that labs have published.

The pages in this section describe each part of the interface. The four pages you use every day come first; the others are grouped by task.

### Everyday screens
{: .no_toc}

- [__Dashboard__]({{"webinterface/dashboard/"|absolute_url}}): The landing page, with widgets you choose and arrange, a getting-started checklist and the recent activity feed
- [__Submission forms__]({{"webinterface/forms/"|absolute_url}}): Tabbed forms with chained selectors, type-specific fields, the materials editor, coordinates, templates and autosave
- [__List view__]({{"webinterface/list-view/"|absolute_url}}): Table views with search, configurable filters and columns, the active projects working set, sorting, pagination and bulk actions
- [__Single entry view__]({{"webinterface/single-entry-view/"|absolute_url}}): The details page of an entry with its header buttons, related entries and history

- [__Exports and reports__]({{"webinterface/exports-and-reports/"|absolute_url}}): Print, Word and spreadsheet exports, project data downloads and the STAR Methods report
