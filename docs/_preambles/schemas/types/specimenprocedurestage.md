## AdministrationStage
Records one stage of a substance delivery to the animal within an administration procedure, such as induction or maintenance of an anesthetic, with route, delivery method, gas concentration, volume, infusion rate and duration.

## SpecimenProcessingStage
Records one stage of a specimen procedure, such as an incubation or wash, with contact duration and temperature, agitation, number of repeated cycles and bath volume.
