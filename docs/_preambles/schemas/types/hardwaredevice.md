<!-- Introductions for the HardwareDevice schema types, used by scripts/build_reference_docs.py when the schema itself has no description. One "## group: <group name>" section per group (exact group name from the input), one "## <TypeCode>" section per type (exact code). -->

## Amplifier
An amplifier product for electrophysiological signals, described by its nominal specifications such as channel count, gain range, bandwidth, input impedance and noise level. Individual units in a lab's setups are documented as equipment that refers to this entry.

## AnesthesiaSystem
An anesthesia system product, such as an isoflurane vaporizer or complete inhalation anesthesia station, with its nominal specifications and supplier.

## AntiVibrationTable
An anti-vibration or optical table product used to isolate setups from floor vibrations, with its nominal specifications and supplier.

## BehaviorRig
An integrated behavioral apparatus sold as one product, such as an operant chamber or commercial task system, with its nominal specifications and supplier.

## BiosafetyCabinet
A biosafety cabinet product for handling viral vectors, cell cultures and other biological materials, with its nominal specifications and supplier.

## Camera
A camera product used for behavioral video or scientific imaging, with its nominal specifications and supplier.

## Computer
A computer model used to run acquisition, stimulus-control or analysis software, with its nominal specifications and supplier. Single-board computers and microcontrollers have their own types.

## DataAcquisitionSystem
A data acquisition product, such as a DAQ board or multichannel recording system, that digitizes analog and digital signals, with its nominal specifications and supplier.

## ElectroencephalographySystem
An EEG system product for recording electrical brain activity from scalp or skull-surface electrodes, with its nominal specifications and supplier.

## ElectromyographyMachine
An EMG system product for recording the electrical activity of muscles, with its nominal specifications and supplier.

## ElectronicComponent
An electronic part or module, such as a connector, relay, filter or circuit board, with its nominal specifications and supplier. Use it for electronics that no more specific hardware type describes.

## EphysRig
An integrated electrophysiology system sold as one product, such as a complete patch-clamp or extracellular recording rig, with its nominal specifications and supplier.

## FiberPhotometrySystem
A fiber photometry system product that delivers excitation light through optical fibers and records fluorescence from genetically encoded indicators, with its nominal specifications and supplier.

## FloatingAirPlatform
An air-supported platform product, for example an air-lifted arena for head-fixed navigation or an air-floated isolation surface, with its nominal specifications and supplier.

## ForcePlate
A force plate product that measures ground reaction forces, for example for gait or startle analysis, with its nominal specifications and supplier.

## FumeHood
A fume hood product for working with volatile chemicals such as fixatives and solvents, with its nominal specifications and supplier.

## GlassMicropipettePuller
A micropipette puller product for fabricating glass pipettes for patch clamp, sharp-electrode recording or microinjection, with its nominal specifications and supplier.

## HumidityController
A humidity controller product for regulating humidity in housing areas, setups or enclosures, with its nominal specifications and supplier.

## HumiditySensor
A humidity sensor product for monitoring relative humidity in experimental or housing environments, with its nominal specifications and supplier.

## InjectionSystem
An injection system product, such as a nanoliter injector or microsyringe pump, for delivering viral vectors, tracers or drugs into tissue, with its nominal specifications and supplier.

## IontophoresisStimulator
An iontophoresis current source product for applying drugs or tracers through micropipettes, with its nominal specifications and supplier.

## Laser
A laser product used for optogenetic stimulation, photostimulation or imaging, with its nominal specifications, such as wavelength and output power, and supplier.

## LedDriver
An LED driver product that powers and modulates LEDs, for example for optogenetic light delivery, with its nominal specifications and supplier.

## LightEmitter
An LED or other non-laser light source product used for optogenetic stimulation, visual stimulation or illumination, with its nominal specifications and supplier.

## LightSensor
A light sensor product, such as an ambient light sensor or optical power meter, with its nominal specifications and supplier.

## MagneticResonanceImagingSystem
An MRI scanner model for structural or functional imaging, with its nominal specifications and supplier.

## MagnetoencephalographySystem
An MEG system model for recording the magnetic fields generated by neural activity, with its nominal specifications and supplier.

## Magnetometer
A magnetometer product for measuring magnetic fields, for example for head-orientation tracking, with its nominal specifications and supplier.

## Microcontroller
A microcontroller board, such as an Arduino model, used for task control, triggering or sensor readout, with its nominal specifications and supplier.

## Micromanipulator
A micromanipulator product for positioning electrodes, pipettes or optical fibers with micrometer precision, with its nominal specifications and supplier.

## Microphone
A microphone product for recording sound, including ultrasonic vocalizations, with its nominal specifications and supplier.

## Microscope
A microscope product, such as a widefield, confocal, two-photon or dissection microscope, with its nominal specifications and supplier. Head-mounted miniature microscopes have their own type.

## Miniscope
A head-mounted miniature microscope product for imaging neural activity in freely moving animals, with its nominal specifications and supplier.

## Monitor
A display product used for presenting visual stimuli or operating acquisition software, with its nominal specifications and supplier.

## MotionTrackingSystem
A motion tracking system product that records the position or posture of subjects, for example with infrared cameras and markers, with its nominal specifications and supplier.

## NoiseIsolationChamber
A sound-attenuating chamber product that shields setups from external acoustic noise, with its nominal specifications and supplier.

## OphysRig
An integrated optical physiology system sold as one product, such as a complete imaging rig, with its nominal specifications and supplier.

## OpticalCoherenceTomography
An optical coherence tomography system product for depth-resolved, label-free tissue imaging, with its nominal specifications and supplier.

## Oscilloscope
An oscilloscope product for displaying and measuring electrical signals, with its nominal specifications and supplier.

## PerfusionSystem
A perfusion system product for transcardial perfusion or superfusion of tissue preparations, with its nominal specifications and supplier.

## Photodetector
A photodetector product, such as a photodiode or photomultiplier tube, that converts light into an electrical signal, with its nominal specifications and supplier.

## RunningWheel
A running wheel product on which subjects run, often with an encoder for measuring locomotion, with its nominal specifications and supplier.

## SignalProcessingUnit
A signal processing product that filters, conditions or processes signals, for example in real time for closed-loop experiments, with its nominal specifications and supplier.

## SingleBoardComputer
A single-board computer model, such as a Raspberry Pi, with its nominal specifications and supplier.

## SinglePhotonEmissionComputedTomography
A SPECT scanner model for imaging the distribution of gamma-emitting radiotracers, with its nominal specifications and supplier.

## Speaker
A speaker product for delivering auditory stimuli, including ultrasonic sounds, with its nominal specifications and supplier.

## StereotaxicFrame
A stereotaxic frame product that holds the subject's head in a fixed coordinate system for targeting brain regions, with its nominal specifications and supplier.

## StimulationDevice
A stimulator product, such as an electrical stimulus generator or stimulus isolator, with its nominal specifications and supplier. Use it for stimulation hardware that no more specific hardware type describes.

## SurgicalPowerTool
A powered surgical tool product, such as a dental drill used for craniotomies, with its nominal specifications and supplier.

## TemperatureSensor
A temperature sensor product for measuring body, bath or enclosure temperature, with its nominal specifications and supplier.

## ThermalController
A temperature controller product, such as a feedback-controlled heating pad or bath heater, with its nominal specifications and supplier.

## Treadmill
A treadmill product, motorized or freely driven, on which subjects walk or run, with its nominal specifications and supplier.

## UltrasoundImagingSystem
An ultrasound imaging system product, used for example for functional ultrasound or anatomical imaging, with its nominal specifications and supplier.
