# Tutorials
{: .no_toc}

Step-by-step guides for BrainSTEM, from your first project to exports and AI tools. Each tutorial stands on its own and ends with suggested next steps.

## Getting started

- [**Get started!**]({{site.baseurl}}/tutorials/get_started): Create an account, join a group, and create your first project, subject and session.
- [**Manage Groups**]({{site.baseurl}}/tutorials/managing-groups): Create a lab group, invite members and assign manager and owner roles.
- [**Manage Projects**]({{site.baseurl}}/tutorials/managing-projects): Project permissions, membership, publications, exports and the STAR Methods report.

## Lab setup

- [**Lab Infrastructure**]({{site.baseurl}}/tutorials/setting-up-lab-infrastructure): Setups, equipment, inventories and consumable stocks, including catalog probe designs and templates.
- [**Behavioral Assays**]({{site.baseurl}}/tutorials/behavioral-assays): Define the behavioral tasks your sessions refer to.
- [**Managing Data Storage**]({{site.baseurl}}/tutorials/managing-data-storage): Describe where your data files live and link storage locations to sessions.
- [**Resources & Taxonomies**]({{site.baseurl}}/tutorials/submit-resource-and-taxonomies): Add consumables, hardware devices, species, strains and publications to the shared catalogs, with RRID and DOI lookups.

## Workflows

- [**Ephys Workflow**]({{site.baseurl}}/tutorials/electrophysiology-workflow): A theta maze study with a silicon probe implant, its experimental interface, sleep and behavior sessions.
- [**Ex vivo workflow**]({{site.baseurl}}/tutorials/specimen-workflow): Specimen sets derived from a subject, specimen procedures with stages and materials, specimen logs and a specimen-based session.
- [**2P Imaging Workflow**]({{site.baseurl}}/tutorials/two-photon-imaging-workflow): A visual cortex study with a virus injection, a cranial window and two-photon imaging sessions.

## Sharing and export

- [**Share data publicly**]({{site.baseurl}}/tutorials/sharing-project-publicly): Make a project and its personal attributes public, and what the public portal shows.
- [**Exporting to NWB**]({{site.baseurl}}/tutorials/exporting-to-nwb): Download a project or session export and convert it to NWB files with brainstem-nwb.

## AI and automation

- [**Working with AI**]({{site.baseurl}}/tutorials/working-with-ai): The in-app assistant, the MCP server for Claude and Cursor, and the agent skills for coding agents.
